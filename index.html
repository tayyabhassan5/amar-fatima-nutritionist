About the Dietitian

Nutrition care that fits your real life

Every plan starts with your history, your lab reports and your routine — then works with the food already in your kitchen. No imported ingredients, no starvation menus, and no supplement-first shortcuts.

Consultations cover a full dietary assessment, body measurements, a review of your medical history and medications, and a written plan you can actually follow. Follow-up visits adjust the plan as your body, labs and schedule change.

FAQs

Questions patients ask first

Still unsure about something? Send it on WhatsApp before you book — short questions are answered free.

Ask a question
Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Online consultations are available by prior appointment for patients outside Sahiwal. You'll be asked to share your measurements and recent lab reports beforehand, and the plan is sent to you in writing afterwards.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Any lab reports from the last three months (CBC, HbA1c, lipid profile, thyroid, vitamin D, uric acid — whatever you have), your current prescriptions, and a rough idea of what you eat on a normal day. If you've followed a diet plan before, bring it along.

Will I be put on supplements or medicines?

Food comes first. Supplements are only discussed where a deficiency is documented or a doctor has already prescribed them, and prescribing medication stays with your treating physician.

Will the plan use foods I actually eat?

Yes — plans are built from roti, rice, daal, seasonal vegetables, local fruit, dairy and everyday proteins, adjusted to your household's cooking and budget. No imported ingredients are required.

How soon will I see results?

That depends on your starting point, medical conditions, medication and how closely the plan is followed, so no specific number is promised. What is agreed up front is a safe, steady rate of change and a follow-up schedule to track it honestly.

How often are follow-ups needed?

Usually every two to four weeks at the start, then spaced out as things stabilise. Follow-ups are where the plan gets adjusted to your progress, tolerance and changing routine.

Can children and pregnant women be seen?

Yes. Maternal and child nutrition is a core area — pregnancy and breastfeeding diets, weaning, fussy eating, growth concerns and anaemia. Care is coordinated with your gynaecologist or paediatrician where needed.
